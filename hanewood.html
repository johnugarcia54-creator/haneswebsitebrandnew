01 · MDF

MDF

Smooth to the core.

Medium-density fibreboard with a fine, even face and a consistent core. It machines cleanly, routes crisply and paints beautifully: made for joinery, cabinetry, shelving and furniture.

02 · HDF

HDF

Denser. Harder. Thinner.

High-density fibreboard packs more fibre into every millimetre for a hard-wearing, stable panel: made for cabinet backs, drawer bases, door skins and more.

03 · Plywood

Plywood

Inside every sheet

Strength, crossed.

Plywood starts as thin veneers of timber, peeled from the log and laid up one on top of the next.

Cross-laminated

Every other layer, turned 90°.

Each veneer's grain runs across the one below it. That keeps the sheet stable, stops it splitting and makes it strong in both directions.

Bonded

Heat. Pressure. One solid sheet.

The veneers are glued and pressed into a single panel: structural plywood to AS/NZS 2269.

H3.2 treated

Made for outside.

Radiata pine plywood treated to hazard class H3.2 for exterior use above ground, out in the weather.

LVL

Line them up, and it's LVL.

Turn every veneer the same way and you get laminated veneer lumber: long, straight and strong along its length. LVL to AS/NZS 4357.

LVL. Long, straight, strong.

Structural LVL to AS/NZS 4357.

LVL

Beams you can build on.

Laminated veneer lumber for beams, lintels and headers: straight, consistent from one length to the next, and stocked ready for your yard.
