The Bargainhub idea

Rooms like these usually come with a showroom markup. Bargainhub designs the whole interior with you, then supplies it [warehouse-direct:] kitchens, bathrooms, joinery and lighting, drawn to fit, chosen to match and delivered on [one order.]

Design studio

Drawn to the millimetre, before anything is made.

  1. Measure and plan

    Every wall, window and door measured and drawn to scale.

  2. Elevations

    Each wall drawn front-on, with heights, hoods and LED strips marked.

  3. Materials and finishes

    Laminates, quartz, tile and hardware chosen to sit together.

  4. 3D model

    The whole room modelled, so you can see it from every angle.

  5. Finished render

    The room lit and styled, the way it will look when it is done.

  6. Your drawing set

    Plans, elevations, finishes and a render, before you commit.

Designed with you

Your ideas, drawn up properly.

Bring a sketch, a photo or just a feeling. We turn it into plans and a 3D model, then supply every panel, benchtop and fitting to make it real.

  • Kitchens, bathrooms, laundries and living spaces
  • Plans, elevations and a 3D model of the room
  • Finishes chosen to match from room to room
  • Supplied together from our Hornby warehouse
