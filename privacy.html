2. What we collect and why

  • Enquiries. When you send an enquiry or a quote request, we collect your name, email, and anything else you choose to give (phone, business, region and your message), so we can reply and follow up on it.
  • Accounts. If you have an account: your name, email and, if you add it, your phone number. Your password is held by our sign-in provider, never by us.
  • Designs and files. Designs, projects and files you save to your account, so you can come back to them and we can quote and supply them.
  • Bookings. If you ask to book a consultant, the details you give, so a consultant can contact you to confirm a time.
  • Consultant intake. Details a Bargainhub consultant records with you, with your agreement, so we can help with your project.
  • Questions to Milli. If you type a question to Milli, our guide, we use it only to answer you. We don’t keep the text.
  • Technical data. A scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address, our session cookie, sign-in events (such as when you signed in) and the browser storage described in section 9. We use these to keep accounts secure and the site working.

You don’t have to give us your details, but without a name and email we can’t reply to an enquiry.

4. Our legal position

Each provider above holds your information on our behalf, as our agent (Privacy Act 2020, section 11). That means passing it to them is not a disclosure under information privacy principle (IPP) 12, and we stay responsible for keeping it safe (IPP 5). We tell you about them up front, as IPP 3 asks. [To be confirmed in legal review]

5. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and leads: 24 months after our last contact with you, including in our customer system.
  • Accounts: while the account is active, plus 24 months.
  • Accepted quotes, signatures and the audit log: 7 years.
  • Server logs: 30 days.
  • Backups: 13 months.
  • Milli statistics: 13 months, with no message text.
  • The copy we keep while handing details to our customer system: reduced after 90 days.

6. Marketing

We only send you news and offers if you tick the box that asks for them. Every message has an unsubscribe link. Replies to your own enquiry are not marketing (Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007).

7. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you or to correct it (IPP 6 and IPP 7), or ask us to delete it. We reply within 20 working days. Deletion covers our server, our sign-in provider (Supabase) and our customer system (Base44). Each enquiry is also emailed to our enquiry inbox, and we delete it there too. Copies in our encrypted backups are not changed, and expire within 13 months. [To be confirmed in legal review]

Until you can do this yourself in your account, email our privacy officer (see section 1).

8. Security

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest. Staff can only see what their role needs. Backups are encrypted and kept off site. We are adding two-step sign-in for staff.

10. Using Milli, our guide

  • Milli’s answers are general information. A person confirms prices, timing and terms.
  • Please don’t share personal details with Milli. Use the enquiry or booking form instead.
  • No unlawful or abusive use.
  • Milli is for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, please ask a parent first.

11. Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please contact us first (see section 1). You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.
