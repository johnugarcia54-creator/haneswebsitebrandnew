What's inside every roll?

Inside every roll

Millions of tiny pockets of still air.

Glass wool is fine glass fibre, spun into a soft blanket that traps still air, and still air is what slows heat down. More wool, more trapped air, a higher R-value. That's how the range climbs from R2 to R7.
